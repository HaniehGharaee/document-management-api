# Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

The goal of this project is to build a platform for managing documents, projects, tasks, and internal communication within companies and organizations.

The platform will provide a centralized place to store and organize company documents, manage projects and employee responsibilities, track deadlines, generate periodic reports, and support communication between team members, including remote employees.

## 2. Actors

The system has three primary actors:

- **Company Manager:** Responsible for managing company activities, overseeing projects, assigning tasks, and monitoring employee progress.
- **Employee:** Uses the platform to access relevant documents, participate in projects, manage assigned tasks, and communicate with other team members.
- **System Administrator:** Manages the platform at the system level, independently of individual companies. This role is responsible for overseeing registered companies and managing the overall system.

## 3. Problems to Solve

The platform aims to address the following problems:

- Difficulty storing, organizing, and accessing company documents in a centralized location.
- The risk of losing files or having difficulty retrieving them when needed.
- Missed deadlines and forgotten tasks.
- Limited visibility into project progress and employee responsibilities.
- Difficulty tracking performance and preparing weekly or monthly reports.
- Communication challenges between team members, particularly when employees work remotely.

## 4. Main Capabilities

The planned capabilities of the platform include:

- User management and authentication.
- File and media management.
- Document storage and organization.
- Project management and project membership.
- Task creation, assignment, and tracking.
- Deadline management and periodic reporting.
- Communication between team members, including video calls.
- System administration and company management.

## 5. Initial Scope

Development will begin with the core backend features, including authentication, user management, file management, media management, and system administration.

Additional capabilities, such as project and task management, search, pagination, advanced permissions, notifications, and real-time communication, will be introduced incrementally in later versions.

The detailed requirements, user permissions, data models, and API specifications will be defined and refined during the design and development process.
